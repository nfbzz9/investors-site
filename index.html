Monthly search volumes: slow-feeder 8,500 · foldable 2,050 · elevated 1,550 · lick mat 600. Even the strongest product caps out at CHF 1,658 per month on Search.

Why revenue can exceed this ceiling

  • The ceiling applies to Google Search only, which is 70% of the channel mix
  • Meta and Snapchat generate demand rather than capture it: not bounded by keyword volume
  • Repeat customers and email carry no acquisition cost at all
  • Complementary products on a winning site widen its keyword pool and raise its own ceiling
Validation

At the stated 70% Google share, the reference operator spends about CHF 918 per site per month: inside the independently calculated saturation range of CHF 120 to 1,658. Two independent methods converge, validating both the calculation and the observed strategy.

Why year 2 dips

The dip reflects the step increase in structure costs (logistics, tools, accounting) crossing the CHF 150k revenue threshold before the winner base has grown enough to absorb it. Cost steps used: tools and accounting below CHF 150k of revenue, 3PL and part-time help below CHF 400k, one full-time employee below CHF 800k, then a proportional team.

Beyond year 5

Winners begin retiring after five years, offsetting new ones. Sustained growth then requires a higher test cadence or expansion into Germany and Austria: same language, direct reuse of catalogue, pages and creatives, at roughly ten times the search volume. Noted as the primary long-term growth path, not modelled here.

12 · Exit Strategy

Four return paths,
in sequence or in parallel.

01

Operate for cash flow

The default. A mature portfolio of 6 to 10 winners generates roughly CHF 90k to 150k of annual net result with limited ongoing capital requirement.

02

Sell individual sites

Profitable niche stores trade at 24 to 40 times monthly net profit on Flippa, Empire Flippers or Acquire.com. A winner netting CHF 1,900/month is worth about CHF 46k to 76k. Selling ageing winners recycles capital into new tests.

03

Sell the portfolio

A strategic buyer (an established pet retailer or an aggregator) acquires the full portfolio: supplier relationships, keyword positions and operating know-how. Portfolio sales typically command higher multiples than single sites.

04

Expand, then exit

Building the same portfolio across Germany and Austria before sale substantially increases both revenue and strategic value: the acquirer inherits a proven multi-market operation.

Realistic timeline: options 1 and 2 are available from year 2 onward. Options 3 and 4 require meaningful scale, so year 4 at the earliest.
